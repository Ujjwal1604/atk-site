📦 Download Website Files

Follow these steps to get the built website

Step 1: Click the button below to go to Netlify
Step 2: Log in with your email (if asked)
Step 3: Click "Site configuration" in the top menu
Step 4: Click "Deploys" in the left sidebar
Step 5: Click on the latest deploy (top of the list with green ✓)
Step 6: Scroll down to "Deploy summary" and click the three dots menu (⋮), then "Download deploy"
Open Netlify Dashboard →
💡 Important: The downloaded file will be a ZIP containing the public folder with all website files. Extract it and upload the contents of the public folder to your institute server at /atk/.
